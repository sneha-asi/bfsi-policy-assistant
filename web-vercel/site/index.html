Assistant
Ask a question about a policy and you will get a direct answer, together with the exact document and page it came from. Answers are drawn only from the loaded policy documents. If something is not covered, the assistant says so instead of guessing. Type your question or ask by voice with the mic button (speech is transcribed with Whisper). This demo is loaded with sample policies for a fictional insurer, "Aegis Financial"; the documents are synthetic. You can also add your own policy PDFs with "Upload" - they are processed entirely in your browser.
